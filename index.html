First-draft structure

One dashboard, organised around the decisions an operations manager must make.

FIRST-DRAFT INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE / READ TOP TO BOTTOM
01 / Act first

Needs attention

Emergency alerts, equipment risk, staffing gaps and price recommendations sit at the top because these require immediate action.

02 / See the floor

Screen status

A five-screen view makes normal, turnaround and halted states scannable at a glance.

03 / Make a decision

Pricing + occupancy

Suggested price movement is paired with house occupancy so Ravi can assess the recommendation before applying it.

04 / Keep coverage

Staffing + daily pulse

Coverage, revenue, ad inventory and footfall provide context without distracting from urgent work.

The annotated screen comes first; the four cards then explain the logic behind each zone for a recruiter or reviewer.
Final direction · Google AI Pro

A detailed revenue and demand strategy dashboard for deeper decision-making.

GOOGLE AI PRO / FINAL HIGH-FIDELITY DASHBOARD DIRECTION
